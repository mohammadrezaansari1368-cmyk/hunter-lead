# معیار لید واجد شرایط آرومین

A living rulebook. Rules tagged (baseline) come from current practice; rules tagged (rep) come from sales-rep feedback and override baseline when they conflict. Merge new feedback into the right section instead of appending duplicates.

## 1. Minimum bar (all must be true)
- A reachable phone number exists; a job-ad poster who listed a phone counts as a lead. (baseline)
- Real, operating business in the target region: Rasht, then Lahijan, then Bandar Anzali, then the rest of Gilan, then neighbouring provinces. (baseline)
- Not blacklisted, not a duplicate of an existing CRM contact or open deal. (baseline)
- A real, related need for what we sell, verified before handoff; a scraped business listing alone is not a lead. Quality and relevance beat volume. (rep)
- Target market only: restaurants, cafes, shops/retail that need POS, Sepidz software, pagers, paper rolls, or store hardware. Verify the number actually answers before handoff. (rep)
- A concrete need or gap (no system, old/third-party system, growth, consumables running out) and a realistic purchase chance. (rep)
- Likely to answer the phone: prefer numbers already seen responding (e.g. replied on Bale, active ad poster) over cold directory numbers. (rep)

## 2. Buying signals (raise score)
- Hiring a cashier, salesperson, hall staff, or accountant: growth, shifts, and money handling. (baseline)
- New branch, multiple branches, or long two-shift hours. (baseline)
- Hiring staff "skilled in sales software" means they use or need a POS. (baseline)
- Consumables reorder: business already has a system (even a competitor's, e.g. Dasht) and uses thermal paper rolls; asking paper price / "send a proforma" = fast-win consumables lead. (rep)
- Newly opened cafe/restaurant (easy to find on social media) or posting job ads in Telegram job channels: preferred software leads. (rep)
- Per-shift sales commission means they need shift sales reports. (baseline)
- Pre-opening is the best time to contact a new cafe/restaurant, but the buying cycle is long: opening delays keep pushing the deal, so plan steady follow-ups from intent-to-open until actual opening. (rep)
- Instagram page signals: looks established, not very small, and shows management has invested in the place (decor, quality). (rep)
- Rep-nominated Instagram leads: when a rep forwards an Instagram reel or page of a cafe/restaurant, treat it as a top-priority lead. Pull the manager name, phone and address from the page bio or highlights, dedupe against the CRM, then create the contact and a deal in Joolio (CRM) assigned to that same rep so they can call. (rep)

## 3. Red flags (drop or downgrade)
- No findable phone, or only a login-gated number. (baseline)
- Contact says they don't recognise us and shows no interest: neutral, leave for manual follow-up, no new deal. (baseline)
- Unreachable / never answers, or business outside our field and target market: drop. These were the most common failures in early handoffs. (rep)
- Just installed a complete POS/accounting suite (sales, accounting, inventory), already has tablet POS and a paper-roll supplier, and no pager need: not a software lead now (no Sepidz/pager pitch); still eligible as a consumables (paper-roll) lead if they don't have a locked-in supplier. Check for a recent install before handing off. (rep)
- Very small one-person cafe (owner alone, busy mornings): not ready for software; they'd hire staff first, then buy software. Downgrade to later follow-up. (rep)
- A job ad by itself is not a lead for us; it counts only with a real POS/software signal (cashier, sales software, shifts, growth). (rep)
- Hypermarkets/supermarkets for software offers: avoid; they usually already have software or need third-party software we can't support ourselves. Only pitch our own Sepidz software. (rep)

## 4. Scoring hints
- Score axes: fit, intent, timing, authority, engagement, reach, data, strategic; class B or higher goes to reps. (baseline)
- Prefer a named owner or manager (decision-maker) over a generic contact. (baseline)
- In cafes/restaurants the owner decides on software purchases; reach the owner. Best call time differs per business, no fixed rule. (rep)
- Most common objection from cafes: price ("too expensive"); prepare value/installment answers. (rep)
- Before handoff, self-check each lead: does the number answer, is the business in our market, is there a visible gap our product fills (no system, old system, growth)? ARASH must check the outcomes of its own referred leads in Joolio (CRM) and learn from it instead of waiting for reps to report. (rep)

## 5. Product fit by business type
- Restaurant, cafe, fast food, catering: Sepidz POS + QR Menu (+ thermal printer for heavy take-away). (baseline)
- Shop, hypermarket, clothing, building supplies: Sepidz POS (store mode) + barcode scanner + customer club; chains add Sepidz Web. (baseline)

## 6. What reps want more or less of
- Less: bulk lists of unqualified businesses, unreachable numbers, unrelated business types. (rep)
- More: fewer, verified leads with a real related need, valid contact data, and a realistic chance of converting. (rep)
